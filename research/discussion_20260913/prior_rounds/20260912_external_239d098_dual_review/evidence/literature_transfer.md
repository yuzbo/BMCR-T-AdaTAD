# Literature-transfer evidence (primary paper pages)

Scope: targeted mechanism checks requested by the parent review. These are paper-level transfer boundaries, not a SOTA survey. No claim below establishes THUMOS accuracy.

## Evidence

- **AdaFocus V2 (arXiv:2112.14238v2)** — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.14238) says it is for *video recognition*, reformulates AdaFocus as one-stage end-to-end training using differentiable interpolation-based patch selection, and adds conditional exit for temporal adaptive computation without additional training. The paper claims experiments on ActivityNet, FCVID, Mini-Kinetics, Something-Something V1/V2, and Jester. Transfer support: adaptive spatial crop/local computation plus optional temporal exit. Boundary: it does not by itself justify deleting RGB input or preserve accuracy on a different benchmark/task.

- **SlowFast (arXiv:1812.03982)** — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.03982) explicitly defines a Slow pathway at low frame rate for spatial semantics and a Fast pathway at high frame rate for fine temporal motion; Fast can be lightweight by reducing channel capacity. It reports action classification/detection on Kinetics, Charades, and AVA. Transfer support: high-frequency temporal branch with low channel budget. Boundary: it remains a two-path video model and does not show that a single RGB stream can be removed, nor guarantee another dataset's accuracy.

- **QueryDet (arXiv:2103.09136)** — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.09136) says coarse small-object locations are predicted on low-resolution features, then accurate high-resolution detection is computed sparsely guided by those positions; it is a feature-pyramid detector for high-resolution small-object detection. Transfer support: dense/coarse FPN stage followed by sparse high-resolution head. Boundary: this architecture assumes the dense/coarse feature pipeline; it is not evidence that RGB frames or the upstream modality can be discarded.

- **PointRend (arXiv:1912.08193)** — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.08193) (paper title: *PointRend: Image Segmentation as Rendering*) describes treating segmentation as rendering, selecting points adaptively for point-wise fine prediction, and training/inference point selection strategies. Transfer support: coarse prediction plus selective point refinement. Boundary: point sampling differs between training and inference; this is an image segmentation mechanism, not proof of free temporal/state reuse.

- **RandLA-Net (arXiv:1911.11236v3)** — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.11236) states the method uses random point sampling and compensates for accidental loss of key features with a local feature aggregation module that progressively increases receptive fields. It targets large-scale point-cloud semantic segmentation (up to 1M points). Transfer support: sampling must be paired with local aggregation/geometric context. Boundary: the input is 3D point clouds and the task is per-point semantics; it does not transfer as a claim about RGB video windows without task-specific validation.

- **3DSSD (arXiv:2002.10187)** — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10187) describes a one-stage, anchor-free 3D detector and a feature-guided sampling strategy combining geometric and semantic feature information to retain foreground points. Transfer support: mixed geometry/feature-guided sampling. Boundary: this is LiDAR/3D object detection; it cannot establish RGB temporal-sampling accuracy.

- **Deep Feature Flow (arXiv:1611.07715)** — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.07715), also [CVPR primary PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2017/papers/Zhu_Deep_Feature_Flow_CVPR_2017_paper.pdf), states that the expensive convolutional sub-network runs only on sparse key frames and deep feature maps are propagated to other frames using a flow field; end-to-end training improves recognition accuracy. The CVPR abstract says validation is on video object detection and semantic segmentation. Transfer support: key-frame heavy feature extraction plus motion/flow propagation. Boundary: propagation incurs flow computation and temporal drift/error; this does not prove a free cross-window state cache or THUMOS preservation.

- **Eventful Transformers (arXiv:2308.13494)** — [arXiv/HF paper mirror](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.13494) states subsequent inputs are compared for temporal redundancy and only significantly changed tokens are reprocessed; models can often be converted without retraining and report 2–4x savings with minor accuracy reductions on ImageNet VID and EPIC-Kitchens 100. Transfer support: update gate over changed tokens. Boundary: the mechanism is comparison against the previous update plus retained token state; state reuse is not equivalent to free reuse across arbitrary windows, and reported reductions include accuracy tradeoffs.

- **Expediting Large-Scale Vision Transformer (arXiv:2210.01035v1)** — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01035) explicitly targets dense prediction without retraining/fine-tuning. It clusters neighboring tokens to lower-resolution representations, applies later transformer layers to clustered tokens, then reconstructs high-resolution tokens; it evaluates five dense prediction tasks. Transfer support: clustering/reconstruction can accelerate an already trained ViT without fine-tuning. Boundary: it is token-space dense prediction and does not establish no-fine-tuning transfer to video action recognition or cross-window state.

- **ETAD (arXiv:2205.07134v2)** — the requested paper/version was not reliably retrieved from the search results in this pass. Treat the external wording “training cost/partial gradients” as requiring a direct paper check; in any event, a training-gradient saving claim is not evidence of reduced RGB inference cost unless the paper explicitly measures that path.

- **Temporal Corruptions (arXiv:2403.20254v1)** — the requested arXiv page was not reliably retrieved in this pass. The specific claim that mid-action perturbations can be more severe therefore remains unverified here and should be labeled as pending primary-source confirmation.

## Incremental value of the newly added SlowFast and Deep Feature Flow references

SlowFast adds a concrete temporal architecture principle: preserve a low-rate semantic stream while adding a high-rate, low-channel motion stream. Deep Feature Flow adds a different mechanism: skip the expensive feature backbone on non-key frames and propagate key-frame deep features using learned flow. Both are useful mechanism analogies for compute reduction, but neither paper supports a blanket claim of THUMOS accuracy preservation, RGB elimination, or cost-free state reuse.
