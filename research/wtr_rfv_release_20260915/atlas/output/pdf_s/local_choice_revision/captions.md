## fig2_task_necessity

All 211 videos and 792 windows per model. Video-balanced estimates and 95% video-cluster intervals. Observation replacement, spatial FFN choices, layer-local refinement choices and fixed-K frame exchanges are distinct conditional quantities. No-op and benign 160-to-158-to-160 image resampling controls are shown. The red line is the video-balanced 95th percentile of absolute benign effects, estimated on this same publication cohort after the original analysis; it is an empirical perturbation reference, not pure numerical noise or a significance threshold. The original 1% tolerance remains dotted gray. Percentages strictly above the benign reference are reported; uncertainty, including re-estimation of the threshold, is in numeric_tables.md. Top-5/10/20 cells give exact positive-effect shares and 95% video-cluster intervals. Concentration is computed on the positive part; the no-op has no positive mass, so its Lorenz/top-p curves are undefined. Panel (d) uses the existing benign-reference band: orange/blue mean task loss increases/decreases beyond the band, while gray means the normalized change lies within it. This is a descriptive tolerance, not statistical equivalence or unchanged detection mAP. Raw signed fractions are preserved in the appendix. T changes one selected frame at fixed K384. D/S change one token slot from light to heavy at a specified layer while other slots remain full. S switches only the FFN; D switches attention-plus-FFN refinement, retaining TIA. token counts stay fixed but FLOPs increase. Other compute choices stay fixed, while downstream features and predictions are recomputed. O replaces one RGB frame and is a separate input probe, so it is excluded from (d). O retains L(replaced)-L(dense); D/S retain L(light)-L(heavy); T retains L(base)-L(exchanged). No stored sign is flipped. Signed raw effects and sample-weighted estimates remain unchanged. Frame-exchange curves use eligible choice sets: S has 187 eligible videos. Per-axis video and measured-change counts are included in the source data.

## fig2_joint_interventions

Effects of jointly applied local changes versus the sum of measured one-change effects. Every combination was actually executed, and the recorded legal composition/order is preserved. Low-effect choices are ranked by absolute effect magnitude, not signed value. Solid and dashed lines distinguish joint measurements from additive approximations. The lower row explicitly shows joint-minus-sum interactions and 95% video-cluster intervals. These are within-axis O/D/S coalitions, not cross-axis interactions. Annotations give the k=16 mean and 95% CI; both orders select the same full 16-change collection there. All sizes/orders are tabulated in numeric_tables.md. Failure to reject zero interaction is not proof of additivity. No global additivity or submodularity claim follows.

## fig3_proxy_value_ranking

All 211 videos and 792 windows. D-axis diagnostics rank the effects of 16 distinct one-token layer-local refinement choices per window; top 20% means k=4. The finite actual-value ranking has Spearman/NDCG=1 and regret=0. Random lines are exact expectations over random orderings; bands/whiskers are 95% video-cluster intervals. NDCG uses positive relevance; regret keeps signed task-loss values and equals the top-k actual-value sum minus the proxy-selected sum. These first-order combinations are not re-executed, and this metric does not include STOP or substitute for RFV single-swap decision regret. Paired proxy-minus-Random results and coverage are in numeric_tables.md. Some proxies capture top-k positive relevance despite little global rank correlation.

## fig4_allocation_s

Frozen AdaTAD-S, all 211 videos/792 windows. Every plotted budget is actually executed and evaluated with full-dataset AP. One-token changes from light to heavy layer-local refinement: TAD-conditional effects are separate from population sampling. Classification and localization effects, duration strata, and proxy ranking quality are reported without assuming boundary dominance. Intervals resample videos; duration cutoffs come from training annotations. Intervals for individual groups are not paired tests of between-group differences. ## appendix_raw_signs

The original video-balanced sign fractions are preserved without a tolerance band. Gray means an exactly zero stored effect; orange and blue include arbitrarily small negative and positive values. O stores L(replaced)-L(dense), so positive O means replacement increases task loss. D/S store L(light)-L(heavy), and T stores L(base)-L(exchanged). Therefore a common sign color does not assign a common benefit direction across all rows. These sign counts alone do not establish useful computation, proxy quality, or changes in detection mAP.
